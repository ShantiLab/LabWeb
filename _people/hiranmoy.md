## Biography

Hiranmoy Adak is a dedicated PhD Scholar at IISER Berhampur, specializing in Biophysics and Structural Biology. His research focuses on understanding complex biological systems using advanced biophysical and structural biology techniques. He completed his Master's in Biotechnology from the Regional Centre For Biotechnology and his Bachelor's in Microbiology from Ramakrishna Mission Vidyamandira.

Hiranmoy has been recognized for his academic excellence with achievements such as the DBT BET JRF (Category I) and an AIR-7 in GATE Life Science 2023. He is actively involved in the scientific community, collaborating on projects that integrate biophysical methods with bioengineering approaches to address fundamental questions in biology.
