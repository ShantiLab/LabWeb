## Biography

Shraddha Dhuware is a Project Junior Research Fellow at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), Berhampur, working remotely from Brahmapur, Odisha. She joined IISER Berhampur in September 2023, where she contributes to ongoing research projects in her field.

She holds a postgraduate degree in Biotechnology from Dr. Harisingh Gour Central University Sagar (M.P.), completed in 2019. With over four years of experience, she has worked as a Laboratory Assistant in UG PG Lab at IISER Berhampur and as a Project Lab Technician in a COVID-19 Lab, where she was recognized as the "Best Employee of The Year 2021" for her exceptional contributions. Her expertise includes Molecular Biology, Molecular Cloning, RNA Isolation, and Real-Time PCR.
