## Biography

Dr. Avishek Kar is a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at IISER Berhampur, specializing in Structural Biology. He recently joined in December 2024 to work under Dr. Shanti Swarup Srivastava, focusing on advanced structural biology techniques. Avishek successfully defended his PhD thesis in November 2024 at CSIR-IMMT Bhubaneswar, where he developed innovative self-assembled DNA nanomaterials for regulating gene expression in cancer cells.

With expertise spanning molecular biology techniques and nanotechnology, Avishek has contributed to pioneering research in cancer therapeutics. His work integrates materials science with biomedical applications, demonstrating exceptional skills in experimental design and biophysical characterization. An active sports enthusiast, he enjoys volleyball and maintains a balanced approach to academic rigor and physical wellness.
