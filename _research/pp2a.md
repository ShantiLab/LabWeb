## Project Overview

Protein Phosphatase 2A (PP2A) is a critical regulatory enzyme involved in maintaining cellular balance through its role in dephosphorylation. It is essential for processes like cell cycle control and signal transduction.

This project aims to provide a generalized understanding of PP2A's structural and functional properties using a combination of computational and experimental techniques. Our focus is to explore its overall role in cellular regulation without revealing detailed mechanisms.

## Key Objectives

- Assess the overall structural features of PP2A.
- Explore its general role in cellular regulation.
- Examine its interactions with other cellular proteins.
- Evaluate its potential implications in disease contexts.

## Significance

Gaining a generalized understanding of PP2A is vital for appreciating its role in maintaining cellular homeostasis and its potential as a target in therapeutic research.
